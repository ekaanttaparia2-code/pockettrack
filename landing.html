❓ Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PocketTrack really free?+
Yes, completely free. No hidden charges, no premium tiers, no ads. Your data stays on your device.
How does voice entry work?+
Just tap the microphone and speak naturally in English or Hindi. Say something like 'spent 200 on lunch' and PocketTrack logs it automatically.
Can I install PocketTrack on my phone?+
Yes! PocketTrack is a Progressive Web App (PWA). On Android, open in Chrome and tap "Install App". On iPhone, open in Safari, tap Share, and choose "Add to Home Screen".
How do I delete my account and data?+
Go to Settings → Reset & Clear All Data. This permanently deletes everything from your device. If you're signed in to cloud sync, your cloud data is also wiped.
Is my financial data secure?+
Your data is stored locally on your device by default. If you enable cloud sync, it's encrypted and stored in your private Firebase partition — no one else can access it, not even us.
What if I lose my phone?+
If you enabled cloud sync before losing your phone, just sign in on any browser and your data will be restored. If you were using guest/offline mode only, the data on that device cannot be recovered.
