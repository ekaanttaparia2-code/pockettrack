❓ Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PocketTrack really free?+
Yes, completely free. No hidden charges, no premium tiers, no ads, and no locked features. Your data stays on your device.
Can I see category breakdowns like Groceries vs. Dining Out?+
Yes, absolutely. PocketTrack features 8 dedicated categories out of the box—including separate categories for Groceries 🥦 and Food & Dining Out 🍔, plus Shopping, Transport, Bills, Entertainment, Health, and General. The Insights dashboard shows you exact percentage breakdowns and category progress bars every month.
Can I export my data to PDF or CSV?+
Yes! PocketTrack includes 1-click bank-style PDF statement generation and clean CSV export. You can download your complete transaction history anytime for tax filing, budgeting spreadsheets, or offline archiving.
How does the Safe-to-Spend budget work? Is it just a simple average?+
No, it is a dynamic daily rebalancing algorithm: (Available Balance - Monthly Savings Target - Fixed Bills) ÷ Days Remaining in Month. If you overspend today, tomorrow's daily allowance automatically adjusts downwards to protect your savings goal without breaking your plan.
Does PocketTrack support recurring bills and subscriptions?+
Yes. Under the Recurring Rules tab, you can automate recurring monthly expenses like rent, gym memberships, Netflix, or utility bills. PocketTrack automatically logs them on the scheduled date so your budget is always accurate.
How does PocketTrack scale if I have thousands of transactions?+
PocketTrack is built on high-performance browser IndexedDB storage, capable of storing over 50,000+ transactions with blazing-fast ~10ms query times. Your data is stored locally in binary indexed records, so the app remains silky smooth even after years of continuous daily use.
Why manual and SMS logging instead of automatic bank sync?+
Deliberate privacy design. Traditional budgeting apps require you to hand over your net-banking credentials or SMS inbox permissions to third-party aggregators who sell your financial profile to loan brokers. PocketTrack protects your privacy: you log transactions in 2 seconds via Quick Composer, Voice, or 1-tap clipboard paste from UPI SMS (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), with zero bank access required.
How does voice entry work?+
Just tap the microphone and speak naturally in English or Hindi. Say something like 'spent 200 on groceries' and PocketTrack logs it automatically to the right category.
Can I install PocketTrack on my phone?+
Yes! PocketTrack is a Progressive Web App (PWA). On Android, open in Chrome and tap "Install App". On iPhone, open in Safari, tap Share, and choose "Add to Home Screen".
How do I delete my account and data?+
Go to Settings → Reset & Clear All Data. This permanently deletes everything from your device. If you're signed in to cloud sync, your cloud data and all subcollections are also wiped.
Is my financial data secure?+
Your data is stored locally on your device by default. If you enable cloud sync, it's encrypted and stored in your private Firebase partition with strict per-user security rules — no one else can access it, not even us.
What if I lose my phone?+
If you enabled cloud sync before losing your phone, just sign in on any browser and your data will be restored instantly. If you were using guest/offline mode only, you can restore from an exported backup file.
Who built PocketTrack and what is the support commitment?+
PocketTrack was created by Ekaant Taparia as an open-source, community-first financial tool. The entire codebase is public on GitHub, completely free of venture capital pressure, paywalls, or advertising tracking. Support is actively maintained via direct email (ekaanttaparia2@gmail.com), our Contact Page, and GitHub Repository Issues.
