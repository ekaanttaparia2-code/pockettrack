PocketTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Introduction & Our Core Philosophy

At PocketTrack, we believe your personal finances are your business and nobody else's. We designed PocketTrack to be client-first and transparent. We never sell your data, we do not partner with third-party ad brokers, and we do not run intrusive tracking scripts.

2. What Personal Information We Collect

When using PocketTrack, we collect only the minimum data required to deliver core functionality:

  • Account Identifiers: Your email address and Google user ID when you voluntarily sign in to enable real-time multi-device cloud synchronization.
  • Financial Entries: Amounts, transaction dates, descriptions, categories, and wallet identifiers that you explicitly enter, speak via voice log, or paste from UPI payment notifications.
  • Split Ledger Records: Names and balances of friends you choose to track in the P2P Debt Ledger or shared Spaces.

If you use PocketTrack in Guest Mode, zero account data is sent to our servers. Everything stays strictly in your browser's private sandboxed storage.

Zero Analytics & Zero Trackers: PocketTrack does not load or initialize Google Analytics, Crashlytics, Mixpanel, or third-party telemetry scripts. In Guest Mode, no background telemetry or transaction data is ever transmitted.

3. Why We Collect It & How It Is Used

We use your data solely to:

  • Calculate your daily Safe-to-Spend runway and wallet balances in real-time.
  • Synchronize your transaction ledger across your phone, tablet, and laptop via encrypted Google Firebase.
  • Generate local charts, monthly spending insights, and PDF statements.
  • Format 1-tap WhatsApp payment reminder links that you choose to send to friends.

We never use your financial activity for advertising profiling, credit checks, or algorithmic data selling.

4. Storage, Security & Data Ownership

You own 100% of your data.

  • Local Persistence: In Guest/offline mode, records and preferences are stored locally in your browser's sandboxed localStorage for lightning-fast responsiveness, with Firestore offline caching utilizing browser IndexedDB.
  • Screen Privacy Shield: The optional 4-digit PIN Privacy Shield provides a visual screen-masking layer to hide sensitive balances and transaction values from shoulder-surfers in public spaces. It is an on-screen obfuscation barrier, not cryptographic device filesystem encryption.
  • Cloud Encryption: When cloud sync is enabled, records are transmitted via TLS 1.3 encryption directly to Google Cloud Firestore with authenticated, per-user security rules ensuring only your account can access your data.
  • Full Data Export & Safe Wipe: You can export your complete financial history at any time as a CSV or JSON file, or permanently erase all local and cloud records with two-step typed confirmation in Settings.
