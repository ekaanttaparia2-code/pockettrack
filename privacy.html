PocketTrack Privacy Policy — Plain-Language Data Protection

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Introduction & Our Core Philosophy

At PocketTrack, we believe your personal finances are your business and nobody else's. We designed PocketTrack to be client-first and transparent. We never sell your data, we do not partner with third-party ad brokers, and we do not run intrusive tracking scripts.

2. What Personal Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in using Google or email/password, we collect your email address and display name. If you use PocketTrack in Guest Mode, no account or email information is ever collected.
  • Financial & Transaction Entries: Any transaction amounts, categories, dates, wallet assignments, notes, and savings goals that you log into the application.
  • Voice & Clipboard Data: If you use Voice Logging or UPI SMS Paste, your audio or pasted text is processed directly on your device via the browser Web Speech and Clipboard APIs to extract transaction figures. We do not store or transmit raw audio recordings.

3. Why We Collect This Information

  • To Calculate Your Balances & Budgets: To show your net balance, compute your Daily Safe-to-Spend limit, and provide monthly spending insights.
  • Optional Multi-Device Cloud Sync: If you choose to log in with an account, your transaction records are synced securely to your private database partition on Google Firebase so you can access your data across phones and laptops.
  • To Generate Your Financial Statements: To create and download PDF and CSV financial summaries locally on your device upon your request.

4. Where Your Data Lives & How It Is Protected

Guest Sessions: 100% of your data lives strictly in your browser's local sandbox (LocalStorage and IndexedDB). It is never sent over the network to any remote server.

Cloud Sessions: Authenticated data is transmitted using TLS 1.3 encryption to Google Cloud Firestore with security rules ensuring that only your authenticated account can access your data.

Data Erasure: You can delete all your records at any time using the "Reset & Clear All Data" button in Settings, or download an encrypted JSON backup file.
